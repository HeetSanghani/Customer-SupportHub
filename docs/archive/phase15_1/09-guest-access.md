# 09 - Guest User Handling
## Policy & Behavior
- Unauthenticated guest chats are tagged with .
- Public RAG queries (knowledge base articles, shipping policies, product listings) are allowed.
- Private account/order/payment queries trigger the Guest Guard notice:
  *"You are currently chatting as a Guest. To view your personal order, payment, shipment, return, or refund details, please log in to your authenticated ShopKart account."*

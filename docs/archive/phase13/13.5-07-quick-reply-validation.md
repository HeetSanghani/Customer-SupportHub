# 13.5-07 — Quick Reply Validation

## Overview
Validates Admin Quick Reply management and priority resolution engine.

## Findings
- **Precedence**: Quick Replies match intent keywords (e.g. "Hello", "Thank you") but defer to specific entity queries ("Thanks, what is status of ORD-025?").
- **Admin CRUD**: Tested `GET`, `POST`, `PATCH`, `DELETE` endpoints under `/api/v1/quick-replies`.
- **Toggle Verification**: Disabling a quick reply immediately stops keyword triggering in chat.

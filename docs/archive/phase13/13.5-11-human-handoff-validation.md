# 13.5-11 — Human Handoff Validation

## Overview
Validates automated escalation from AI Assistant to Human Agents.

## Trigger Statements Verified
- "I want to speak to an agent."
- "This is unacceptable. I want a supervisor."
- "I will take legal action."

## Escalation Results
- Ticket status updated to `Escalated` / `Open`.
- Notification broadcast to Admin Global Inbox.
- AI Assistant enters passive monitoring state.

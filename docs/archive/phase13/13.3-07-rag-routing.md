## Retrieval Architecture (`RAGPipeline`)
- **Chunking Engine (`DocumentChunker`)**: Splits documents into paragraph chunks (max 300 words with 50-word overlap).
- **Embedding Provider (`LocalVectorEmbeddingProvider`)**: TF-IDF keyword and bigram vectorizer with Inverse Document Frequency weighting and L2 normalization.
- **Cosine Similarity Retriever**: Ranks chunks by cosine similarity score against query embedding. Top $K=3$ chunks retrieved.

---

## Policy Grounding & Response Generation
- Retained chunks formatted as: `[POL-001: Return & Exchange Policy]\n<chunk text>`.
- Citations appended to response metadata: `{"source_id": "POL-001", "title": "...", "score": 0.8542}`.
- LLM Response Generator uses system prompt enforcing strict adherence to retrieved text to prevent hallucinated policies.

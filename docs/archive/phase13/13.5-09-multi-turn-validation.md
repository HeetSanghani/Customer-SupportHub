# 13.5-09 — Multi-Turn Context Validation

## Overview
Validates multi-turn context isolation, entity reference resolution, and domain switching.

## Multi-Turn Flow Matrix
- Turn 1: "What is the status of ORD-025?" -> Sets `active_order_id = ORD-025`.
- Turn 2: "Can I cancel it?" -> Implicit "it" resolved to `ORD-025`.
- Turn 3: "What is the price of PROD-002?" -> Domain switch to Product context; clears active order slot.
- Turn 4: "Where is my shipment?" -> Fetches active shipment for customer `CUST-001` without order bleed.

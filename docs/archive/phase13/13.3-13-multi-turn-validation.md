# Phase 13.3-13: Multi-Turn Context Validation

## Overview
This document reviews the 5 canonical multi-turn domain switching sequences remediated during Phase 13.2.6.

---

## 5 Multi-Turn Test Sequences

### Sequence A: Order → Product → Product Follow-up
1. Turn 1: `"What is the status of ORD-025?"` -> Context: `active_order_id = ORD-025`.
2. Turn 2: `"Tell me about PROD-002."` -> Context: `active_product_id = PROD-002`, `active_order_id = None`.
3. Turn 3: `"What is its price?"` -> Resolves to `PROD-002`.

### Sequence B: Product → Order → Order Follow-up
1. Turn 1: `"Tell me about PROD-002."` -> Context: `active_product_id = PROD-002`.
2. Turn 2: `"What is the status of ORD-025?"` -> Context: `active_order_id = ORD-025`, `active_product_id = None`.
3. Turn 3: `"What is its total?"` -> Resolves to `ORD-025`.

### Sequence C: Product → Shipment → Product
1. Turn 1: `"Tell me about PROD-002."` -> Context: `active_product_id = PROD-002`.
2. Turn 2: `"Where is my shipment?"` -> Context: `active_shipment / order` resolved.
3. Turn 3: `"What is its price?"` -> Uses `last_product_id` fallback to resolve to `PROD-002`.

### Sequence D: Order → Policy → Order
1. Turn 1: `"What is the status of ORD-025?"` -> Context: `active_order_id = ORD-025`.
2. Turn 2: `"What is your return policy?"` -> RAG route executed.
3. Turn 3: `"What is its total?"` -> Resolves to `ORD-025`.

### Sequence E: Unauthorized Order → Implicit Follow-up
1. Turn 1: `"What is the status of ORD-003?"` -> Access Denied. Context cleared.
2. Turn 2: `"What is its status?"` -> Returns "unable to locate requested record". Zero leak.

---

## Technical Mechanism Summary
- **Domain Slot Isolation**: Switching to a new domain clears active slots from unrelated domains.
- **Historical Entity Fallback**: `last_product_id` and `last_order_id` enable same-domain follow-ups after temporary domain switches.
- **Zero-Trust Security Guard**: Denied or unauthorized entity lookups trigger `revert_slots()` so unowned IDs never enter context history.
